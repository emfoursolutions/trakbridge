What is TrakBridge?

TrakBridge is a data bridge for TAK (Team Awareness Kit) environments, connecting tracking services, intelligence feeds, and tactical systems into a unified operational picture. It pulls real-time positional data from GPS tracking services, OSINT feeds, and location APIs. It also accepts data pushed from devices and external systems over HTTP, MQTT, WebSocket, and UDP multicast; and delivers Cursor-on-Target events both into and out of TAK servers.

The platform is built around an extensible plugin system comprised of input, inbound, and output plugins that share a published SDK. This allows new integrations to be added without touching the core. Automated stream lifecycle management, encrypted credential handling, and fully offline operation make TrakBridge suited to everything from connected cloud deployments to air-gapped tactical networks.
